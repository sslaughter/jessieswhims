# Instructions for AI assistants

These instructions apply to the entire repository.

## Personal authorship

This is Jessi's personal blog. Preserve her ownership of its voice and creative content.

- Do not generate, add, rewrite, expand, or substitute blog content without explicit direction from the user to do so.
- Content includes text, titles, taglines, descriptions, reflections, bouquet names and recipes, Bible verses, songs, Chinese idioms, translations, images, illustrations, audio, video, and other creative material. This includes metadata, alt text, sample entries, and placeholder copy.
- A request to build, style, fix, organize, or deploy the website does not authorize creating content. Do not infer permission from a topic, the site's theme, missing content, or an empty section.
- Use user-supplied content as provided. Ask before editorial changes, selecting media, or filling gaps. Leave gaps empty or use non-rendered technical comments when needed.
- When content generation is explicitly requested, stay within the requested scope. Do not add other creative material.
- Technical implementation, layout, accessibility structure, bug fixes, and deployment may proceed within the user's request, provided they do not invent or alter personal content.
- Technical documentation and repository instructions may be written when requested or necessary to explain authorized technical work; they must not introduce blog content or attribute invented statements to Jessi.
